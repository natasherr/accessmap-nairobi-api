REFLECTION

The interviews were more useful than expected. We initially struggled to see
a connection between an accessibility directory and a waste marketplace.
However Team 13 pointed out that our accessibility badges — specifically
ramps, wide corridors and accessible parking — directly determine whether
a waste collection vehicle can access a venue. That was something we had
never considered. We also identified that our venue data could serve as
suggested meeting points for waste buyers and sellers, providing safe and
accessible locations for transactions to take place. On the upstream side,
connecting to Voter Edu required creative thinking but venues like schools
and community halls in our directory could realistically serve as voter
education centers, making the connection workable.
